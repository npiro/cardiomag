About the physics

Simulated sources: activation from an anisotropic eikonal model (validated against the reference biatrial simulations, mean error ≈1 ms) with a template action potential; primary current J = −σᵢ∇Vₘ with an effective intracellular conductivity calibrated to the measured PTB P-wave (Kcal = ). Current dipoles are lumped into 8–9 mm voxels; B at each sensor is computed here by Biot–Savart. The normal component is exact for a flat (half-space) chest; tangential components omit volume currents. Measured sources are median beats from the PTB SQUID and Kiel OPM recordings on their own layouts (time relative to the R peak).
